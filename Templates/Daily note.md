## Plan

Written by the brain before 07:30. Six lines: the one thing today is for, two more if there is room, one risk or deadline inside seven days, one decision waiting, one suggestion, missed runs only if any.

Yours: what you are committing to today, most important first. Pick tasks on the platform with Today or a Suggested button, or write them here, one line each in the form `- Task words · [[Note it lives in]]`. A line without a link is a one-off for the day.
