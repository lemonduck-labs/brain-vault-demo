# Gaugewell commercial model

How Gaugewell might make money. Drafted, **not priced**: no price is set until the internal trial shows the five reports are worth paying for.

## Who pays

Owner-run service businesses spending on ads every month, with one person who reads the numbers on a Monday. The same buyer Owlbridge serves, which is the point: an Owlbridge client is the first likely Gaugewell customer.

## Shape of the plans

| Plan | Who for | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| Solo | One location, one or two ad accounts | The five reports, weekly |
| Group | Several locations or brands | The five reports per brand and combined |
| With Owlbridge | An Owlbridge retainer client | Included in the retainer |

Prices not set. The questions that set them are below.

> [!danger] Open problem
> If Gaugewell is included in Owlbridge retainers, it earns nothing on its own until there are clients who are not Owlbridge's. That may be the right trade for the first year. [Guessing]

## What sets the price

1. What the five reports save an owner in time each week, from the trial.
2. What it costs to run per client (hosting and the model calls for report 5).
3. Whether owners would pay monthly or only alongside a retainer.

## Decisions

- **14 Oct** No price before the trial. Ruled out a launch price on the waitlist page, which would anchor a number nobody has tested.
