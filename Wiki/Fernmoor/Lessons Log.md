# Lessons Log

What went wrong or right at Fernmoor and the rule it left behind, newest first. Added to, never rewritten.

### 22 Oct 2026, two tools, two lead counts

The Hub counts a form fill or an answered inbound call as a lead; [[Gaugewell]]'s trial counts form fills only. The same week showed two different lead totals and both were right. **Rule:** every lead figure says which count it uses, and the director report uses the Hub's.

### 21 Oct 2026, two PRs on one module

PR 39 merged first and touched the export module PR 41 also changed, so 41 needed a rebase before it could merge. **Rule:** when two open PRs touch the same file, say so in both and merge the smaller first.

### 19 Oct 2026, a figure the managing director could not check

The Warmstead step was agreed but held because nobody could say whether the 47 leads included calls. **Rule:** a figure that will move money is shown with how it was counted.

### 12 Oct 2026, broad terms in a new pilot

Warmstead's first week brought grant-seekers on two broad terms. **Rule:** a new retrofit campaign launches with grant and scheme negatives on day one.

### 18 Sep 2026, numbers from a connector

Three times a connector's figure disagreed with the ad platform and the Hub. **Rule:** ad figures come from the [[Fernmoor Hub]] only. Written into [[Corrections]].

### 15 Sep 2026, a time promise without the installs manager

A draft ad promised surveys "this week" without checking capacity. Caught before it ran. **Rule:** time promises go through the installs manager; it is in the [[Advertising claims checklist]].
