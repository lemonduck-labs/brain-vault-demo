## Who does what

Roles only. The detail is in [[Agencies and partners]] for anyone outside the group.

- **The managing director**: budget, sign-offs, the GDPR call on call content.
- **The operator, head of growth**: paid acquisition for all three brands, the CRM, call-team reporting, the [[Fernmoor Hub]], the weekly director report.
- **The sales manager**: the sales team and the CRM task types.
- **The call-team lead**: the call team, first contact on every lead.
- **The finance lead**: month-end spend and margin.
- **The installs manager**: survey and install capacity, which caps how far any brand can be pushed.
- **The CRM contractor**: the CRM rebuild, on a three-month contract with a break clause.
