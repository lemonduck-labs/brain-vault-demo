# Advertising claims checklist

What every ad, landing page and email claim is checked against before it goes live, for all three brands. It is a working list, not legal advice: anything it cannot settle goes to the managing director.

## Every claim

- Is it true today, and could we show the evidence if asked? Keep the evidence linked beside the copy.
- Is a figure ours, measured, and dated? No borrowed or rounded-up numbers.
- Does "from" or "up to" sit next to the figure it qualifies, in the same size?
- Would a customer reading only the headline come away with the right idea?
- Are savings described as estimates, with what they depend on?
- Is anything about a competitor? If so, it comes out.
- Does a time promise ("surveys within a week") match what the installs manager says the team can do this month?

## Finance and schemes

- Finance wording is the finance lead's approved wording, word for word.
- Never imply a grant or scheme the brand cannot arrange. If unsure whether a scheme applies, leave it out.

## Who signs off

| Kind of claim | Sign-off |
|---|---|
| Prices, finance, guarantees | The finance lead |
| Timings, capacity | The installs manager |
| Anything new or unsure | The managing director |

> [!note] How it is used
> This is the standing checklist. Each launch copies the questions into its own note and ticks them there, as the [[Warmstead launch plan]] did.
