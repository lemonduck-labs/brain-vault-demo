# Warmstead

> [!abstract] In one line
> The group's retrofit brand: loft and cavity insulation and air source heat pumps, piloted in the Midlands, with ads live since Mon 5 Oct 2026.

The newest brand and the one the operator's first goal rests on: a measured CPL of £25 or under by 30 Nov.

## State at a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **First two weeks** (5 to 18 Oct) | Spend £1,560, 47 leads, CPL £33.19, 6 surveys, 1 sold |
| **By channel** | Search £520, 18 leads, £28.89; Meta £1,040, 29 leads, £35.86 |
| **Target** | CPL £25 or under by 30 Nov |
| **Next** | The budget step, £300 a week from Meta to Search, waiting on the operator's yes since 19 Oct |
| **Counts from** | 5 Oct. No older lead is Warmstead |

Figures from the [[Fernmoor Hub]], checked 22 Oct.

> [!important] The bet, 5 Oct
> Retrofit buyers search when they are ready and scroll when they are not. Search should carry the CPL and Meta should build the pool. If that is right, moving spend to Search brings the CPL down without losing surveys. [Likely]

## Why the gap to £25 is closable

1. Search already runs at £28.89 in its first fortnight, with no negatives beyond the launch list. [Likely]
2. Meta's first creative set is three weeks old by 26 Oct and the second set is not built yet. [Certain]
3. Six surveys from 47 leads is a 13% survey rate. Heating runs at about 15%, so the call team's handling is not the main gap. [Likely]

> [!danger] Open problem
> Survey capacity for the pilot in November is not confirmed. A CPL win means little if surveys queue for three weeks.

## Decisions

- **5 Oct** Warmstead counts from zero. Ruled out back-filling older retrofit enquiries.
- **19 Oct** Budget step agreed in principle at the marketing meeting, pending two weeks of CPL. Ruled out a bigger move of the whole Meta budget, which would leave no read on Meta at all.
