# Meta Ads

How the group runs Meta for its three brands, and the rules learned so far. Figures are in the [[Fernmoor Hub]], not here.

## Structure

| Brand | Campaigns | Objective | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fernmoor Heating | Boiler replacement; servicing plans | Leads, instant form | Retargeting of site visitors runs inside the main campaign |
| Ridgeby Roofing | Roofing, North East | Leads, instant form | Small budget; under review once the Search read is in |
| Warmstead | Heat pumps; insulation | Leads, instant form | Since 5 Oct. First creative set three weeks old by 26 Oct |

One ad account for the group, with brand as the first word of every campaign name, per [[Attribution and UTM spec]].

## Rules

- Do not split a thin signal. A campaign still learning cannot fund a second one. [Certain]
- New creative is a new ad in the same ad set, not a new ad set, unless the audience changes. [Likely]
- Every claim on a creative goes through the [[Advertising claims checklist]] before it is uploaded.
- A conversion location change resets learning. Re-check the creatives after any such switch.
