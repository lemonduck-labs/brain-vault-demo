## What sells

- **Boiler replacement**: the volume product. Leads come mostly from Search on "new boiler" and "boiler replacement" terms with a town or region.
- **Servicing plans**: lower value, steadier; best sold to existing install customers rather than bought on Search.

## Rules for this brand

- The finance line on the landing page uses the wording the finance lead approved. Any change goes back to them.
- "Free boiler" and "grant" terms stay negative. They bring scheme enquiries the brand cannot serve (sweep 15 Oct).
- Winter demand lifts from late October. Do not read a CPL rise in the first cold week as a campaign problem without the survey rate beside it. [Likely]

## Decisions

- **15 Oct** Kept Performance Max running while two asset groups are read for another week. Ruled out pausing the whole campaign, which carries a third of the brand's surveys.
