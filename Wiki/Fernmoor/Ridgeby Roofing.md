## The Search restructure

Before 21 Oct one Search campaign carried repairs and replacement together, so a cheap repair lead and a full replacement lead looked the same in the CPL. From 21 Oct:

| Campaign | Covers | Read on 28 Oct |
|---|---|---|
| Repairs | Leaks, slipped tiles, flashing, gutters | CPL and survey rate |
| Replacement | Full and part roof replacement | CPL, survey rate, jobs sold |

> [!warning] A 7-day read is early
> Seven days on a brand with about 58 leads a week is enough to see whether the split broke anything, not enough to judge it. The full read is at three weeks. [Likely]

## Decisions

- **20 Oct** Split Search by job type. Ruled out splitting by town, which would leave each campaign too thin to learn.
- **20 Oct** Added "jobs" and "course" as negatives. Ruled out a broader negative list until the search terms report has a week on the new campaigns.
