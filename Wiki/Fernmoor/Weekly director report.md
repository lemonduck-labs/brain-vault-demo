# Weekly director report

The Monday report to the managing director, at 09:00, covering all three brands from the [[Fernmoor Hub]]. This note holds the format and the latest report; each week's report replaces the last one here, and the figures stay in the Hub.

## Format

1. What needs a decision, first.
2. One table: every brand, then the group.
3. Two or three lines per brand: what moved and why.
4. What happens this week.

No brand is left out. A brand with nothing new still gets its row.
