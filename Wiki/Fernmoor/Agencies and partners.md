# Agencies and partners

Who outside the group works on Fernmoor's marketing and systems, what they own, and the terms that matter. Roles only.

## The CRM contractor

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | A small CRM firm rebuilding the group's CRM in stages |
| **Contract** | Three months, with a break clause |
| **Contact** | Their lead consultant, by the shared project channel |
| **Owns** | Settings and configuration in the CRM, per the stage briefs |
| **Does not own** | The Hub, the ad accounts, the call team's ways of working |
| **Stage** | Stage 1 handed over 20 Oct; stage 2 brief sent 20 Oct |

> [!important] The exit line
> Build so the group can run the CRM without the contractor. The group takes every settings task it can; the contractor takes the work that needs their skills. Every change is written up in plain words at handover. See [[CRM rebuild]].

## No agencies on paid media

Paid acquisition for all three brands is run in house by the operator since the start of the year. Ruled out an agency for Warmstead's launch on 29 Sep: a pilot this small would pay a minimum fee for little attention.

## Platforms

Meta, Google Ads, GA4 and the CRM are accounts the group owns, with the operator as an admin and the managing director as the owner of record.
