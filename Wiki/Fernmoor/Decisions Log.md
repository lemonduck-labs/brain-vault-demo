# Decisions Log

Fernmoor's cross-project decision register, newest first. Each project note keeps its own dated decisions; this note carries the ones whose reasoning applies beyond the project that produced them. Every entry names what it ruled out.

## Judgement rules that came out of decisions

> [!important] Cheap and good are different questions
> A channel with a higher CPL and a better survey rate can be the cheaper channel. Cost per sale is leads per sale times CPL. Applied to Warmstead's step on 22 Oct: Search wins on both, so the step is not a trade-off this time.

> [!important] Do not split a signal that is already thin
> A campaign still learning cannot fund a second one. Ruled out splitting Ridgeby by town on 20 Oct.

> [!important] Count from the day a brand exists
> A new brand's figures start on its launch day. Ruled out back-filling Warmstead on 5 Oct.
