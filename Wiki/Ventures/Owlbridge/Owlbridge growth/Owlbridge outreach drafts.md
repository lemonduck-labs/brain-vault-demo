# Owlbridge outreach drafts

The three messages every Owlbridge lead gets, in order, sent by the operator through the business's own site form. The SDR fills the placeholders and writes the one personal line; the words around it change only here. Voice rules are in [[Owlbridge tone of voice]].

## Email 1, day 1 (under 90 words with the personal line)

**Subject:** {{company}}: where your new enquiries come from

Hi {{contact role}},

{{one line about the business: the new site, the new booking system, the thing that changed}}

Most owner-run practices we speak to spend on ads or listings every month and cannot say which of it brings in new customers. Owlbridge sets that up in a month: every enquiry tracked to where it came from, one page a week that says what is working.

Would a 20-minute call next week be useful?

{{sender}}

## Email 2, day 4 (in the same thread)

**Subject:** Re: {{company}}: where your new enquiries come from

Hi {{contact role}},

A short follow-up. The first thing we usually find is that one channel brings in most of the enquiries that turn into customers, and it is rarely the one with the most clicks.

If that is worth a look for {{company}}, reply with a day that suits.

{{sender}}

## Email 3, day 10 (in the same thread)

**Subject:** Re: {{company}}: where your new enquiries come from

Hi {{contact role}},

Last note from me. If tracking enquiries is not a priority this season, no problem, and I will not write again unless you ask.

If it is, the offer of a 20-minute call stands.

{{sender}}

## Sign-off on every message

{{sender}} is the operator's sign-off for Owlbridge, the consultancy's name, and a line saying how to opt out ("reply 'no thanks' and I will not write again"). An opt-out goes on the suppression list the same day.
