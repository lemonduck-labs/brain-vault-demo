# Owlbridge tone of voice

How Owlbridge sounds in every message, proposal and page. Settled by the operator on 8 Oct, after the first batch's drafts read too much like an agency.

## The reader

An owner or practice manager who runs the business and answers the enquiries. Busy, sceptical of agencies, good at their own trade. They want to know what it costs, what they get, and whether it will take their time.

## The voice

- **Plain.** Short sentences. The words the owner would use: "enquiries", "new patients", "bookings", "what it costs".
- **Specific.** One real thing about their business in the first line, or no first line.
- **Unhurried.** No urgency, no "limited spaces", no "quick win".
- **Honest about size.** Owlbridge is small and says so. That is the point: the owner talks to the person doing the work.

## Never

- Claims about results Owlbridge cannot show. No percentages from other clients until a client agrees to be quoted.
- Comparing on price, or saying anything about a competitor.
- Jargon: "funnel", "ROAS", "omnichannel", "synergy", "growth hacking".
- More than one question in a message.

## Example lines

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| We drive growth through data-led strategies | We track every enquiry back to where it came from |
| Book your free strategy session today | Would a 20-minute call next week be useful? |
| Unlock your marketing potential | One page each Monday that says what is working |
