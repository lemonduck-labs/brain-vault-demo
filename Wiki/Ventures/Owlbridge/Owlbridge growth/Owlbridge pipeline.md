# Owlbridge pipeline

Every Owlbridge lead, one row each. The platform's Leads area and the Director both write here, one row at a time; every touch is a line in [[Owlbridge activity log]]. Research for each lead is in its lead list note, such as [[Owlbridge lead list 2026-10-07]].

**Statuses, in order:** researched, drafted, sent, replied, call booked, proposal, won, lost, opted out, disqualified. Lost, opted out and disqualified are out of play.

**Contact** is a role, never a name. **Email** is blank or "via site form": Owlbridge writes to a business through its own website form, and no address is kept here.

## How a row moves

- **researched to drafted**: the SDR writes email 1 in [[Owlbridge outreach drafts]] wording, with one line about the business.
- **drafted to sent**: the operator sends it through the business's site form. Claude never sends.
- **sent to replied**: any human reply. An opt-out goes straight to opted out and onto the suppression list in [[Owlbridge lead gen station]].
- **replied onwards**: the operator's calls. Reply drafts sit in [[Owlbridge follow-up drafts]].
