## What they asked for

From the discovery call on 30 Sep: new patient enquiries are hard to follow across two sites. The practice pays for a listing site and local search ads and cannot say which brings patients who book.

## What Owlbridge does for them

1. Tracks every new patient enquiry, from the booking form, the phone and the listings, to its source, per site.
2. Sends one page each Monday: enquiries by source and site, how many booked, the cost of each.
3. A monthly review with the owner and the practice manager: what to change.

> [!note] No results claimed yet
> Month one is a baseline. Nothing about Quillmere's figures goes into any Owlbridge outreach without the owner's written yes.
