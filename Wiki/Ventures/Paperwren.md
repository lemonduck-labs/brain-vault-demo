# Paperwren

> [!abstract] In one line
> A small print-on-demand shop selling botanical line-art prints, made and listed by the operator, printed and posted by the print partner.

Low priority on purpose. It earns a little, teaches the operator listing and product photography, and takes about an hour a fortnight.

## How it works

1. The operator draws a print and makes the listing images.
2. The shop lists it on a print-on-demand marketplace.
3. An order goes straight to the print partner, who prints and posts it.
4. The operator answers the odd customer message.

## Decisions

- **7 Oct** Declined a second collection for this year. Ruled out a seasonal set of eight new prints: the time goes to Owlbridge, where one client is worth many months of print sales. Revisit in the [[Twelve month plan]] review in January.
- **2 Aug** Print on demand only. Ruled out holding stock.
