# Owlbridge

> [!abstract] In one line
> A small growth consultancy the operator runs on the side, for owner-run local service businesses: every enquiry tracked to where it came from, and one page a week that says what is working.

The operator's own work, kept apart from Fernmoor. The idea settled on 24 Aug: what the operator does for a group of three brands at Fernmoor, a dental practice or a joiner needs at a fraction of the size, and nobody sells it to them plainly.

## Pricing

Set 8 Oct.

| Plan | Price | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| One location | £900 a month | Enquiry tracking set up in month one, one page each Monday, a monthly review |
| Two or more locations | £1,400 a month | The same per location, plus a combined page |

No long contract: a month's notice. Quillmere Dental, two sites, signed on 2 Oct before the price list was set and keeps its founding rate of £900 until its review on 2 Jan 2027.

> [!important] The bet, 24 Aug
> Owner-run businesses buy from a person they can talk to, on a referral or a well-aimed note, not from an agency pitch. Three clients by 31 Dec proves whether outreach plus referrals can fill the book. [Guessing]

## How leads come in

1. **Outreach**: the [[Owlbridge lead gen station]], driven by [[LEADGEN]]. The operator sends every message.
2. **Referrals**: from clients. Quillmere's owner offered two introductions on 16 Oct.

## Decisions

- **8 Oct** Two prices by location. Ruled out a single price, which undercharges a multi-site practice, and a price per channel, which owners found hard to follow.
- **8 Oct** Cafes leave the target list after batch 2. Ruled out keeping them for volume; the enquiry value is too low for a £900 retainer.
- **27 Sep** Lead gen station v2: Claude drafts, the operator sends through site forms. Ruled out a sending tool on a separate domain for a consultancy this size.
