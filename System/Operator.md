# Operator

The standing profile of the person this vault serves. Two parts, kept apart: **On the record** is only what the operator has said, quoted and dated; **Claude's read** is the brain's own reading, labelled and never treated as fact. WEEKLY and SESSION EXPORT add to it; nothing here is inferred into the first part.

## Role

Head of growth at Fernmoor Home Group: paid acquisition for three brands, the CRM, call-team reporting and the weekly director report. Evenings and weekends: Owlbridge (a small growth consultancy), Gaugewell (a reporting platform) and Paperwren (a print shop).

### Standards

- British English. No em dashes, anywhere. (14 Sep)
- Plain headers. "It's a report, not a pitch." (20 Sep)
- Every ad figure from the Fernmoor Hub, never from a connector. (18 Sep, after the third correction)
- Every group report covers all three brands, never one alone. (25 Sep)
- Warmstead counts from zero on 5 Oct: no older lead is Warmstead. (5 Oct)
- "Check the copy you are planning from is fresh before telling me something did not move." (14 Oct)

### How they read

On the phone at 07:30 and at the desk from 09:00. The daily plan must fit one phone screen. Long detail belongs in the note it links to.

- Raising Paperwren prices this quarter (4 Oct).
- A custom domain for the platform (6 Oct): "The netlify address is fine."
