# Growth

## How it works

- **Suggested**: Claude's suggestions, labelled as such, each with what prompted it and a first step. They stay until accepted or struck.
- **Accepted**: threads the operator said yes to, each with its next action. MORNING may nudge one in the day's Suggestion line.
- **Struck**: kept with the date, so the brain does not suggest the same thing again.
